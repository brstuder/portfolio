# AbbVie Innovation Center Internship

**Digital Communications Intern, *September 2019 - August 2020***

During university, I interned at the AbbVie Innovation Center in Urbana-Champaign, a massive launchpad into multimedia content creation. I operated on several major projects over the course of the annual internship, of which few have survived the test of time.

## Key Projects

* Generated multiple key articles on healthcare concepts for internal sharing on the company portal.
* Distributed monthly newsletters to full-time/corporate AbbVie personnel for an insight into the Innovation Center operations.
* Overhauled AbbVie onboarding materials in Confluence with instructional narrative and video scripts.
