# OpenMRS Fellowship for Google Season of Docs

**Technical Writer/Google Season of Docs Participant, *May 2021 - November 2021***

As part of Google's Season of Docs program ([which Google has since discontinued](https://developers.google.com/season-of-docs)), I worked half a year with OpenMRS an open-source organization working to improve medical record digitalization in areas with poor healthcare infrastructure. Our objective was to improve the efficiency and clarity of new user onboarding, allowing contributors to the organization to swiftly find relevant projects they could lend their talents to.

## Key Projects

### Guide for the New and Curious

My first and most significant contribution to OpenMRS would be a significant overhaul to their [Guide for the New and Curious](https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/25477628/Guide+for+the+New+and+Curious). This is the first stop for new contributors, and we aimed to simplify the onboarding process, and point them to internal teams and projects they could immediately contribute towards.

### Project Page Template

OpenMRS consists of open-source groups and projects which have their own internal communities with team leaders and objectives. I designed this simple [project page template](https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RES/pages/26267259/Project+Page+Template) which allows new teams to get started quickly by allowing them to build their homepages effectively, while also boosting consistency in project pages across the entire organization.
